The table games

Six seats each, so you and your friends can pile onto the same felt and play the same shoe, wheel or shooter.

Blackjack

Six-deck shoe, dealt for up to six players at a seat apiece. A natural pays 3 to 2. The dealer stands on all 17s, soft 17 included, and the shoe reshuffles when it runs low rather than after every hand.

  • Double down on any opening two cards, including after a split.
  • Split once per round on any pair of equal-value cards, so a king and a ten can be split.
  • No insurance and no surrender — neither is offered at these tables.
  • Push on a tie. Bust loses immediately, whatever the dealer does afterwards.
  • Main-floor limits: $25 to $500 a hand.

Craps

A full layout with a live shooter — the dice pass to the next player on a seven-out, exactly as they would at a real table. Everybody at the rail is betting on the same roll.

  • Pass and Don't Pass pay even money. On Don't Pass, 12 is barred — the bet pushes rather than losing.
  • Come and Don't Come travel to their own number the same way.
  • Free odds at true odds, up to 3× your flat bet: 2 to 1 on the 4 and 10, 3 to 2 on the 5 and 9, 6 to 5 on the 6 and 8.
  • Place bets: 9 to 5 on the 4 and 10, 7 to 5 on the 5 and 9, 7 to 6 on the 6 and 8. Off on the come-out, and they stay up after a win.
  • Buy and Lay at true odds, less the standard 5% vig.
  • Hardways: 9 to 1 on the hard 6 and 8, 7 to 1 on the hard 4 and 10.
  • Field pays even money, with the 2 and the 12 both paying double.
  • One-roll props: Any Seven 4 to 1, Any Craps 7 to 1, Aces and Boxcars 30 to 1, Ace-Deuce and Yo 15 to 1.
  • Main-floor limits: $10 to $500 per spot.

Roulette

An American double-zero wheel — 38 pockets, numbered 1 through 36 plus 0 and 00, in the real American pocket order. Green pockets lose every outside bet; there's no en prison or la partage.

  • Straight up 35 to 1 · Split 17 to 1 · Street 11 to 1 · Corner 8 to 1
  • Top line (0-00-1-2-3) 6 to 1 · Six line 5 to 1
  • Dozen and column 2 to 1
  • Red, black, even, odd, 1–18, 19–36 all pay even money
  • Main-floor limits: $5 to $1,000 across the whole board.

Texas Hold'em

Player-against-player no-limit Hold'em, six seats, table stakes — you can shove your whole stack at any point and nobody can bet more than they have in front of them.

  • Entry table: $5 / $10 blinds, $300 buy-in (30 big blinds).
  • A second table in the poker room runs $10 / $20 with a $600 buy-in.
  • The VIP mezzanine table plays $25 / $50 with a $2,000 buy-in.
  • Sit down and your buy-in leaves your bankroll; stand up and your stack goes back into it.

Ultimate Texas Hold'em

The house version, played against the dealer. Post an Ante and a matching Blind, then choose exactly once when to make your Play bet — the earlier you commit, the bigger it can be.

  • Play 4× or 3× your ante before the flop, 2× after the flop, or 1× at the river.
  • Folding is a river-only option and gives up the ante, the blind and any side bets.
  • The dealer needs a pair to open. Without one your ante pushes.
  • The Play bet pays even money when you beat the dealer; ties push.
  • Blind paytable, paid only on a winning hand: royal flush 500 to 1, straight flush 50 to 1, four of a kind 10 to 1, full house 3 to 1, flush 3 to 2, straight 1 to 1. Anything less pushes.
  • Trips side bet pays on your own hand whether or not you beat the dealer: royal 50 to 1 down to trips 3 to 1.
  • A $1 Lucky button pays fixed amounts on your hole cards and the flop, up to $500 for a royal.
  • Main-floor ante limits: $10 to $200.

Video Poker

9/6 Jacks or Better — the full-pay paytable, with a full house at 9 and a flush at 6. Triple Play: run up to three hands off one hold.

  • Straight flush 50 · four of a kind 25 · full house 9 · flush 6 · straight 4 · trips 3 · two pair 2.

Plinko

Drop up to ten balls at once down 12 rows of pegs into 13 buckets. Every peg is a genuine coin flip, so the middle buckets are where most balls land — and they're the ones that pay least.

  • Safe: ×10 at the edges, ×0.5 in the middle.
  • Classic: ×25 at the edges, ×0.4 in the middle.
  • Wild: ×130 at the edges, and the centre bucket pays nothing at all.
  • Bet $5 to $200 a ball on the main floor.

Horse Racing

A six-horse field — Thunderbolt, Lucky Star, Midnight, Comet, Sir Gallop and Dark Horse. Every card deals out the same six prices, shuffled: there's always one 2 to 1 and always one 12 to 1, and the only question is which name is wearing them today.

  • Win pays the horse's posted odds, from 2 to 1 up to 12 to 1.
  • Place (first or second) pays about half those odds.
  • Show (first, second or third) pays about a third.
  • A horse's real chance of winning is set from its price, so the long shot genuinely is one.
  • Main-floor limits: $10 to $500.
