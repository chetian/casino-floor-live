Age Suitability

Last updated: July 25, 2026 · Chetian Holdings LLC

Casino Floor Live! is rated 18+. It is a casino game played with pretend chips. You cannot buy chips, cash them out, or win anything real.

Why we rated it 18+

The game is a casino floor. You play blackjack, poker, roulette, craps, slots, video poker, dice, horse racing, plinko and mines with virtual chips, at a table with your friends.

No real money is involved. Even so, we built a realistic casino, and we think that makes it a game for adults. We gave it the highest age rating available rather than the lowest one we could argue for.

There is no real money in this game

  • Chips are free. You get them when you sit down, and you win more by playing.
  • Chips cannot be bought. We do not sell chips or currency of any kind.
  • Chips cannot be cashed out, traded, or redeemed. They are worth nothing outside the game.
  • There is no real-money betting anywhere in the game.
  • There are no prizes, contests, or sweepstakes. Our leaderboards are bragging rights and nothing else.
  • There are no loot boxes, and nothing random is for sale.

Horror or scary content None.
Bad language None written by us. Players can chat, and we filter it — see below.

Playing with other people

Multiplayer tables have live text chat. It is the only way another player can send you anything, and we keep it in hand:

  • Offensive words are filtered on the sender's phone and again on yours, so a modified app cannot get around it.
  • You can mute or block anyone with one tap. After that you see and hear nothing from them.
  • You can report any message. Reports come to us, we read them, and repeat offenders are removed.
  • Names come from Apple's Game Center. There are no accounts, no profile text, and no way to send a picture.
  • Messages pass between players in real time. We do not store them.

For parents

We made this for adults who want a casino night with friends and no money on the line. It is not for children. Because it carries an 18+ rating, you can block it with Screen Time on your child's device, which stops it being downloaded or opened.

Playing sensibly

There is no money at stake here, so there is nothing to lose. We also left out the tricks that keep people playing: no timers, no daily goals, no streaks to protect, nothing to wait for. Put it down whenever you like and nothing happens.
