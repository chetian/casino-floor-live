Age Suitability

Last updated 25 July 2026 · Casino Floor Live! · Chetian Holdings LLC

Rated 18+. Casino Floor Live! is a simulated casino game. It uses virtual chips that cannot be purchased, cannot be cashed out, and have no real-world value. There is no real-money gambling, no wagering of real currency, and no prizes of any kind.

Why the App is rated 18+

The App simulates a casino floor — blackjack, poker, roulette, craps, slots, video poker, dice, horse racing, plinko and mines — played with virtual chips. Simulated gambling is the central and continuous activity, so we have declared it at the highest frequency available and accepted the resulting 18+ rating.

We chose this rating deliberately rather than minimally. Although the App contains no real-money gambling, we consider a realistic casino simulation to be adult content, and we would rather over-declare than have a younger player encounter it.

No real-money gambling

This is the most important thing a reviewer or parent can know about the App. All of the following are true:

  • Chips are virtual and free. They are awarded at the start of a session and through play. There is no in-app purchase that grants chips, currency, or any gameplay advantage in the form of a balance.
  • Chips cannot be bought. The App has exactly one in-app purchase — a $2.99 non-consumable "VIP" unlock. It grants a badge, a members-only mezzanine, high-limit salon access, and a larger starting bankroll. Nothing about it is redeemable.
  • Chips cannot be cashed out, transferred off-device for value, traded, or redeemed for money, goods, or anything else. They exist only inside the App.
  • There is no real-money wagering at any point, in any mode, on any table.
  • There are no prizes, sweepstakes, or contests with rewards. The App's leaderboards are ranking-only.
  • There are no loot boxes or paid randomized items. The slot bonus rounds are randomized, but they are played with virtual chips and are never purchasable.

User-generated content and chat safety

Multiplayer rooms include live text chat, which is the App's only user-generated content. The following safeguards are built in:

  • A profanity filter runs on the sending device — an offensive word is masked before it ever leaves the phone — and again on the receiving device, so filtering cannot be bypassed by a modified client.
  • Mute and block are available on any player with a single tap. A blocked player's messages, chat bubbles, avatar expressions, and sounds are all suppressed.
  • Reporting is available on any message. Reports reach us by email and are reviewed; repeat offenders are removed.
  • Player names come from Game Center, which supplies its own moderated nicknames. The App has no separate account system, no free-text profile fields, and no way to upload an image.
  • Chat messages are relayed between players in the room in real time and are not stored on our servers.

Who the App is for

Casino Floor Live! is intended for adults aged 18 and over who want the social experience of a casino night with friends without any real-money risk. It is not directed to children. Because it is distributed with an 18+ rating, parents and guardians can restrict it using Apple's Screen Time content restrictions, which will prevent the App from being downloaded or opened on a device configured for a younger age.

A note on responsible play

The App involves no real money and therefore no financial risk. Even so, simulated gambling can be habit-forming for some people. There is no session timer, daily goal, streak pressure, or energy mechanic in the App — a player can stop at any time without penalty, and nothing is lost by not playing. If gambling of any kind is a concern for you or someone you know, the National Council on Problem Gambling maintains a free, confidential 24/7 helpline at 1-800-522-4700.
