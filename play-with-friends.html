How a night on the floor works

There's no lobby screen to stare at and no round timer pushing you along. You walk in and the floor is already running.

1. Invite through Game Center

Sign in to Game Center, pick the friends you want, and send one invite. They land on your floor. There's no account to make, no password, no email address, and no friend code to read out — your player name comes from Game Center and we never store it on our servers.

2. Walk in and split up

Everybody arrives at the same door and then does whatever they like. Someone heads straight for the craps table, someone else circles the slot banks looking for a machine that's hot, someone stands at the bar. You'll pass each other on the carpet.

3. Crowd a table

Sit down wherever there's an open seat. Blackjack, poker, roulette and craps all seat several people at once, so you can pile onto one table and play the same shoe or the same shooter.

4. Leave when you feel like it

No session length, no daily goal, no energy meter. Stand up mid-shoe and go find something else, or stay at one table all night. The room keeps going for everyone still in it.
