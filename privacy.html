Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 7, 2026

The short version: Casino Floor Live! has no ads. On Apple devices it has no trackers, and no analytics or other third-party SDKs. On Android its only third-party SDKs are two of Google's libraries, Play Games Services and Play Billing, which send Google their own analytics and diagnostics. There are no accounts, and we don't collect your email (unless you add it to feedback or email us, so we can reply), contacts, location, or photos. Your player name comes from Apple's Game Center or, on Android, Google Play Games — we don't create it, and we never store it on our servers. When you play online, the app passes your moves and chat messages to the other players in your room through a relay we operate; that relay forwards messages in real time and does not store them. In-game chat is filtered for profanity on your device, and you can mute or report other players. Feedback and reports you send are emailed to us. That's it.

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Casino Floor Live! (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and operated by Chetian Holdings LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), a limited liability company. This policy covers the App on Apple devices and on Android; where the two differ, it says so. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information the App uses

We designed the App to collect as little as possible. Here is everything it touches:

Game Center identity (from Apple)

On Apple devices, your player name comes from Apple's Game Center — the App does not ask you to create a username or password. When you sign in, Apple provides the App with your Game Center display name and a player identifier. Your display name is shown to the other players in your room so people can see who they're playing with. We do not store your Game Center name or identifier on our servers; they exist only on your device and are relayed to the other players in your current room while you play (your name also goes with any feedback or report you send us, as described below). Our relay also keeps a running estimate of how many different players have joined. It is a small aggregate count, not a list of players or a record of anyone's play. Your use of Game Center is also governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Game Center is optional — if you don't sign in, you can still play the App solo, but online invites and multiplayer features will be unavailable.

Google Play Games identity (from Google, on Android)

On Android, your player name comes from Google Play Games instead, and it works the same way: the App does not ask you to create a username or password. When you sign in, Google provides the App with your Play Games display name and a player identifier, and the App uses them just as it uses Game Center's: your display name is shown to the other players in your room, and we do not store your name or identifier on our servers. Google keeps your Play Games profile (your gamer name and avatar) and your leaderboard scores. Your use of Play Games is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and Google describes what Play Games collects in its Play Games Services data disclosure. You can delete the App's game data from your Play Games profile; our Delete your data page shows how. Play Games is optional — if you don't sign in, you can still play the App solo, but multiplayer features will be unavailable.

Google's libraries (on Android)

On Apple devices, the App contains no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. The Android app includes two of Google's libraries: Play Games Services, for sign-in and leaderboards, and Play Billing, for purchases. These libraries send Google their own analytics and diagnostics, which Google handles under its own privacy policy (linked above). The Android app includes no other third-party SDKs.

Multiplayer gameplay data

When you play online, the App sends gameplay messages — such as your player name and color, your seat and table, bets, and game actions — to the other players in your room. This traffic passes through a relay server we operate so that everyone in the room stays in sync. The relay simply forwards messages between the players who are connected at that moment; it treats each message as opaque data, does not maintain user accounts, profiles, or a database of players, and is not designed to store your gameplay or read its contents after it is delivered.

In-game chat

Multiplayer rooms include a real-time text chat so you can talk with the other players at your table. Chat works the same way as other gameplay messages: the text you type is passed through our relay to the other players in your room and is not stored on our servers or kept as chat history after delivery. To keep chat civil:

  • Profanity filter. A built-in word filter masks common profanity and slurs. Filtering happens on your device — both when you send a message (so an offensive word never leaves your device unmasked) and again when a message is displayed to you.
  • Mute and block. You can mute or block any player so you no longer see their messages. Your mute/block list is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Reporting. You can report a message from within the App. Reports reach us by email and include the reported player's name and identifier and the message text. On an iPhone or iPad whose Mail app has an account, and on Apple Vision Pro, Report opens a pre-filled email draft in your mail app for you to send. On Android, and on an iPhone or iPad without a Mail account, the App sends the report through our relay, which emails it to us through Resend, the same way as feedback (below); the relay does not keep a copy. A report sent this way also carries the same app and device details as feedback: your app version and build, your device model and operating-system version, your player name, whether you're a VIP, and your room code. If that send fails, you can email the report to us instead. We review reports and use them only for safety and moderation.
  • House rules. Before chatting for the first time, you agree to keep it respectful — no harassment, hate speech, or abuse.

Feedback

Settings has a Send Feedback form. When you send it, the App passes your note through our relay, which emails it to us through our email provider, Resend. The relay does not keep a copy. Along with your words, the note carries your app version and build, your device model and operating-system version, and, if you have them, your Game Center or Play Games display name, whether you're a VIP, and your room code. The form shows all of this before you send. You can add your email address if you'd like a reply; it's optional, and we use it only to answer you. If you choose to send your feedback by email instead, it goes through your device's mail app.

House shows and bets

The televisions in the App show our own house shows, live for everyone at once. The App downloads the show schedules and results from our relay over HTTPS; those requests don't include your player name or identifier. Bets are placed with in-game chips only. Your bet tickets are kept on your device and settled there — we never store them.

Notifications

Bet-result notifications are optional and off by default. If you turn them on, the App schedules them locally on your device and may check a result in the background when your device allows it; on Android, they're scheduled with the system's alarms, on the device. There is no push server, and nothing about you is sent to schedule them.

Update check

On Apple devices, from time to time the App asks Apple's public App Store lookup whether a newer version has been released. The request carries the App's own identifier and your device's App Store region, and nothing that identifies you. The Android app makes no such check.

Purchases

The App offers optional one-time in-app purchases: a VIP membership and character packs. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit on Apple devices, and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive or store your name, credit-card number, or other payment details — Apple or Google handles all of that. The App only receives a confirmation from Apple or Google Play that a purchase is valid (on Android, Google Play's signed purchase confirmation, which the App checks on your device) so it can unlock what you bought, and checks with Apple or Google Play again when it launches so your purchases are restored.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, chat preferences (including your mute/block list), your bet tickets, and a cached record of what you've bought are stored locally on your device (in your device's app storage). This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us. Deleting the App removes it; on Android, so does clearing the App's storage in Settings › Apps.

Where your information lives — at a glance

  • Your player name & ID — come from Apple Game Center or, on Android, Google Play Games; held on your device and shown to players in your room. Not stored on our servers. Apple or Google keeps your game profile and leaderboard scores.
  • Your moves & chat messages — forwarded in real time by our relay to the other players in your room. Not stored on our servers and not kept as history.
  • Game progress, settings & mute/block list — stored only on your device.
  • Purchases — handled by Apple or Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • House shows — downloaded from our relay; the requests don't identify you. Your bet tickets stay on your device.
  • Player reports you send — reach us by email, from your mail app or through our relay and Resend; not stored on our relay. Used only for safety and moderation.
  • Feedback you send — emailed to us through our relay and Resend; not stored on our relay. Kept in our inbox and used only to answer you and improve the App.
  • Analytics SDKs — none on Apple devices. On Android, Google's Play Games Services and Play Billing libraries send their own analytics and diagnostics to Google.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No phone number, contacts, calendar, or address book, and no email address unless you give us one — by adding it to feedback so we can reply, or by emailing us.

  • No access to your photos, camera, or microphone.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no data brokers.

3. How information is used

The limited information described above is used only to:

  • Let you play with friends and show player names at the table.
  • Keep everyone in a multiplayer room synchronized in real time, including chat.
  • Keep chat civil (on-device profanity filtering, and reviewing player reports for safety).
  • Deliver and restore your in-app purchases (VIP and character packs).
  • Save your progress and preferences on your device.
  • Read the feedback you send, answer it when you've left an address, and fix what it reports.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we never sell it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Information is shared only in these narrow ways:

  • With other players in your room (your display name, chosen color, in-game actions, and chat messages), which is the whole point of multiplayer.
  • With Apple, through Game Center and the App Store, to provide sign-in and purchases under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.
  • With Google, on Android, through Google Play Games and Google Play, to provide sign-in, leaderboards and purchases under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Google's Play Games Services and Play Billing libraries also send Google their own analytics and diagnostics.
  • With Resend, our email provider, only to deliver the feedback and player reports sent through our relay to our inbox.
  • When required by law, if we must respond to a valid legal request.

5. Data retention

On-device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the App (on Android, clearing the App's storage also deletes it). Multiplayer messages are relayed in real time and are not intended to be retained after your session; the relay may keep transient, non-identifying operational logs for a short period to keep the service running and secure. Feedback and player reports you send are kept in our email inbox only as long as we need them to answer you, follow up on what they report, and keep chat safe; we delete yours on request (see Delete your data). Resend keeps its delivery records for a limited period under its own policy. Purchase records are held by Apple or Google Play under their own policies, and your Game Center or Play Games profile and scores by Apple or Google under theirs.

6. Security

Multiplayer connections to our relay server use encrypted transport (TLS/WSS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but because the App collects so little and stores your progress locally, there is very little personal information at risk. Please keep your device and its operating system up to date.
