Delete your Casino Floor Live! data

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Casino Floor Live! is made by Chetian Holdings LLC. The game has no accounts of its own: there's nothing to sign up for, and our servers keep no player records. Here's where your data lives, and how to delete each part.

On your device

Your bankroll, progress, characters, badges, settings, mute list and bet tickets. Uninstall the game to delete them, or open your phone's Settings › Apps › Casino Floor Live! › Storage › Clear storage. On iPhone, iPad or Vision Pro, delete the app. This can't be undone.

Google Play Games

Your gamer name, avatar and leaderboard scores are kept by Google. In the Play Store, tap your profile picture › Settings › General › Play Games profile › Privacy & settings. Under Your data, tap Delete profile & game data, then find Casino Floor Live! under the individual game data and tap Delete. Deleting your whole Play Games profile removes it too. Google's help page Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile has the steps for every device.

Game Center

On Apple devices, Apple keeps your Game Center name and scores. Manage them in Settings › Game Center.

Feedback and player reports you sent us

They reach our inbox by email, and we keep them only to answer you and keep chat safe. To have yours deleted, email contact@chetianholdings.com with the subject "Delete my data", and tell us roughly when you wrote and the player name you used. We delete them within 30 days and reply when it's done.

Purchases

Google Play and the App Store keep the record of what you bought, under their own policies; we never see your payment details. Deleting your game data doesn't cancel or refund a purchase.

Chat and online play

Relayed between players in real time and never stored, so there's nothing to delete.

Questions

Reach us at contact@chetianholdings.com. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
